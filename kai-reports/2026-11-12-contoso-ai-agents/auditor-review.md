# Auditor review — contoso-simulation (Contoso AI agents: evidence.md, risk-register.csv, control-gaps.md)
Overall opinion: Satisfactory with exceptions — the evidence is accurate and I reproduced it, and all five High risks are supported in substance. However, two risk statements go beyond their evidence, one control-gap claim is false, the analyzer's output is treated as a Contoso control, one rationale contradicts its score, and an integrity (fraud) risk is missing.

Review basis (2026-11-12, read-only):
- I re-ran `python3 -B agent_exposure.py inventory`, `attack-paths` and `blast-radius <id>` for all 5 agents from `sc500-ai-agent-security-lab/simulation/`.
- I ran a read-only import of `Environment.attack_paths()` / `choke_points()` from the scratchpad, plus `blast_radius('bp-finance')` (39) and `blast_radius('bp-itops')` (18).
- The outputs match E-53 to E-67 exactly: 11 paths; entry split 6/2/2/1; target split 3/3/2/1/1/1; secret-bp-finance on 4 paths, hidden by the `[:5]` cut.
- I spot-checked the json and py line cites for E-03, E-05, E-07, E-13, E-26, E-30, E-42, E-43, E-47, E-49, E-50, E-60, E-66, E-68 and E-69. All are correct except one small line-number drift (O-13).
- Scores were checked against operator.md (5x5; High 15 or more, Medium 8-14). Every L x I product and rating in the register is arithmetically correct.
- answer-key.md was not used as ground truth. It is compared only in O-14.

## Possible missed risks
- **Payment or invoice fraud through ERP posting:** untrusted email drives the "Post to ERP API" tool with no evidenced approval step. Evidence: E-11, E-13, E-15; ERP API scope and approval step need collection. (Raised as X-07.)
- **Indirect prompt injection into DevOps Copilot through the GitHub platform-runbooks knowledge source:** this route to the R-04/R-05 privileges does not need an assumed-breach workstation. Evidence: E-23, E-24; repository write permissions need collection. Control-gaps already flags it (5.23 row) but no risk exists.
- **Confused-deputy exposure on DevOps Copilot:** tools run as mi-agent-devops for whoever can invoke the agent in Teams. Evidence: E-21, E-22, E-23; agent audience needs collection.
- **Secret values exposed in chat transcripts or logs:** the "Key Vault get secret" tool returns secrets into a Teams conversation, and password resets may return new credentials into the helpdesk chat. Evidence: E-23, E-31; transcript handling needs collection.
- **Unmodelled reach of Contributor on rg-production:** only one `contains` edge (vm-sql-prod) is modelled. Contributor can also act on any other resource in the group (for example, list storage keys, or pivot through a VM's own managed identity). Evidence: E-47; resource group contents need collection.
- **Helpdesk agent used for disruption:** bulk resets or unlocks against many users (availability), and social engineering of the agent. Evidence: E-29, E-31; tool limits need collection.
- **ERP API credential outside the inventory and secret governance:** the agent posts to the ERP, but no credential or permission node exists for it. Evidence: E-15; credential location needs collection.
- **Data retention and privacy of agent conversations and processed content:** anonymous chats with the HR bot, invoice email bodies and attachments processed by the model, and the retention and residency of transcripts. Needs collection. ISO 42001 A.7 and an AI impact assessment under A.5 (verify ID) are not mapped.
- **AI output risk on the public HR Benefits Assistant:** wrong or harmful benefits answers to the public, separate from the data-leak issue. Evidence: E-03, E-05; content evaluation needs collection (NIST AI RMF MAP / MEASURE).
- **Conditional Access and workload-identity protection for agent identities and the blueprint credential:** whether a leaked bp-finance secret can request tokens from any location. Needs collection. This would change the R-04 likelihood.
- **No evidenced incident response or kill-switch for a compromised agent or blueprint:** how to disable the agent, revoke the blueprint credential and revoke tokens. Needs collection.
- **Change control over agent configuration:** who can edit instructions, tools and knowledge in Contoso-Production and the Foundry projects. Needs collection. R-09 covers environment policy only.
- **Currency of the evidence itself:** all four active agents show lastActivity within 2026-09-22 to 2026-09-24 (E-09, E-17, E-25, E-33). That cluster may mean activity telemetry stopped about 49 days ago, or that the model is a stale snapshot. Either reading affects every score. Needs collection: data-source refresh date.
- **bp-itops credential compromise:** blueprint blast-radius score 18 (E-57), storage location unknown (E-51). It appears only as an action inside R-03. Consider an explicit risk or an evidence request entry.
